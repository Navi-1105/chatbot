# Observability

Observability is the ability to understand the internal state of a system by examining its external outputs. The three major pillars of observability are logs, metrics, and traces.

# Monitoring

Monitoring involves collecting and tracking predefined metrics and signals to determine whether a system is healthy. Monitoring is generally focused on known failure conditions.

# Logs

Logs are timestamped records of events generated by applications, operating systems, and infrastructure components.

# Metrics

Metrics are numerical measurements collected over time, such as CPU usage, memory utilization, request rate, and error rate.

# Traces

Traces represent the journey of a request through distributed services. They help identify where latency or failures occur.

# SRE

Site Reliability Engineering applies software engineering principles to operations. SRE focuses on reliability, availability, performance, automation, and reducing operational toil.
